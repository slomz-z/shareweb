Privacy Policy

Effective: August 27, 2026
Version: 2.0
Last Updated: August 27, 2026

1. Introduction & Zero-Knowledge Guarantee

This Privacy Policy explains how ShareWeb ("we", "our", or "the Service") handles your information. ShareWeb is built on a zero-knowledge architectural foundation.

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or profile your personal data. We never have access to the unencrypted contents of your shared files.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimal data points necessary to authenticate accounts and deliver transfers:

  • Account Identity: Email address, display name, profile avatar, and salted scrypt password hash (or Google OAuth sub ID and name).
  • Transfer Metadata: Filename, byte size, MIME type, sender/recipient email identifiers, and completion timestamps (retained for 30 days in your history).
  • Technical & Network Diagnostics: IP address (for localized routing, rate-limit security, and device blocks), WebSocket session state, and essential session cookies.

3. End-to-End Encryption & Cryptography

Every file is encrypted client-side in the sender's browser using AES-256-GCM before transmission. Ephemeral per-file keys are wrapped using public-key cryptography (ECDH P-256).

Zero-Knowledge Guarantee: The server receives, stores, and relays only encrypted ciphertext blobs. The server operator has zero cryptographic ability to inspect, decrypt, or read your file contents.

4. On-Device AI Content Safety

Content safety checks are executed entirely on your device using TensorFlow.js client-side machine learning models.

Files are never uploaded to our servers or third parties for content scanning. Only safety classification results and blocked file hashes are transmitted if a severe policy violation occurs.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Schedule

We follow strict data minimization and automated purge lifecycles:

Data Category Retention Period Purge Mechanism
Account Profile (Email, Name, Avatar) Active Account Lifetime Permanent purge within 48h upon deletion
Transfer Metadata (History) 30 Days rolling Automated rolling expiration
Relayed File Ciphertext Buffers Until delivery (max 7 days) Instant deletion upon transfer completion
Session & Security Cookies 7 Days rolling Automatic session expiration

6. Legal Bases for Processing

Under GDPR and international privacy frameworks, we process data based on contractual necessity (delivering transfers and managing accounts), legitimate interest (securing systems and preventing abuse), and legal compliance.

7. Your Rights & Data Portability

You have full control over your personal data, including the right to access, rectify, download/export your transfer history, restrict processing, and request permanent erasure.

To exercise any of these privacy rights, submit a request via our Support Desk at /support.

8. Cookies, Local Storage & Security

We use secure, HttpOnly session cookies for authentication, and browser IndexedDB/localStorage solely to store cryptographic keys and user UI preferences.

All network communication is enforced through HTTPS and TLS 1.3 encryption in transit.

9. International Transfers & Children's Privacy

ShareWeb is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK). We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

Data transferred across borders is protected by robust encryption and standard data protection contractual safeguards.

10. Contact Us & Support Desk

The server operator acts as the data controller. For questions, data requests, or privacy inquiries, contact our moderation team directly via the ShareWeb Support Portal at /support.
